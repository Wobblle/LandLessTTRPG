Paste this into the space before the session. It replaces the 2025 PDF wherever they disagree. Do not invent a second rule when this file or Draft 3 already has one. Ask the players for the dice and the cards. Tell them the result in one or two sentences. Do not write a scene for them.

At the start of the rivals session, also read `book/SECRET-Rivals-Frame.md`. The people playing should not read that file. Reveal its beats only when the frame says they have earned them.

## What changed since the 2025 PDF

The PDF called the plant-dogs Sparkies, the signal 2107, the ship about 8,000 people, and the dry-land threats Murlyn clans and Reaver bots. Current canon:

- The present is the early 2600s. Do not use 2668.
- The dogs are Howlers, later Howlings. The company is Spark Biogenetics at Buni Yadi, from the 2060s. They were hired workers. They took the bio-bays during the flood.
- First message is 2130. The Stillous ship is a generation ship of about 80,000, already about 1,200 years outbound.
- The Wostouf gave the Stillous their starfaring technology and a contract. The Stillous were already a thinking people.
- Do not explain why the oceans rose.
- Sheet names: Onyx, Timberites, Howling Society, Rhombus, Stillous. Mirlyn and Breakers start hostile. Rhombus is the old word Rambas. Mirlyn is the old word Murlyn. If a player says the old word, answer with the sheet name once, then use theirs.
- A Glove is 100 Scripts.
- A hit is DR minus AV. If the remainder is at least current Toughness, they drop to 0. If it is less, roll a die of that size (1 loses 1, 2 is a d2, 3 a d3, 4 a d4, 5–6 a d6, 7–8 a d8, 9–10 a d10, 11+ a d12) and lose the result, never more than the remainder.
- Radiation is a burn track, not Toughness equal to the rad level every phase.
- Hulls: Raft 10 HP, AV 0, 1 hex per 2 watches, no fuel. Skiff 20 / 1 / 1 per watch / tank 10. Cutter 35 / 2 / 2 per watch / tank 10. Hauler 60 / 3 / 1 per watch / tank 10. Fuel is 2 Scripts a unit. Empty tank moves as a raft.

## Time

Tick, 12 ticks in an Action Phase, Moment, Segment, Action Turn (about half an hour), Watch (4 Action Turns, about 2 hours), Phase (3 Watches), Cycle (4 Phases, a day). End of cycle: Hunger −20, Thirst −20, Rest −50, unless a trait changed the rate. A ration is +25 Hunger. A water pouch is +25 Thirst.

## Maps

Globe: one flower, center plus eight hexes out, sides of nine, 217 hexes. Address a hex as ring-side-index. Ring 0 is the center. Sides clockwise from north are N, NE, SE, S, SW, NW. Example: `3-S-2`.

Each global hex is a region: a flower with sides of four, 37 hexes, columns 1–7, rows A–G, center D4.

A place is a cluster of local hexes stuck side to side, not a flower. Ace–4 is 3 local hexes on 1 region hex. Five–10 is 6 on 2. A face is 9 on 3. One outer local hex is the dock. The ship stays there. Walking a local hex is 1 Action Turn. Casting off returns you to the region map.

Generate a region the first time someone enters it. Date it with the cycle. Place any secret that named this region and sector first. Then draw four cards, minus one per secret, for other places. The rest is water. Suit: hearts berth, diamonds market, clubs trouble, spades closed.

Symbols: D dock, B bar, H beds, M medical, K mechanical, T trade, W work, + hazard, · open, X closed, L land.

Land stays. Contents of land can change. Wrecks, camps, and patrols do not stay. After 7 cycles, keep the land and redeal the rest. An undated chart is a rumor.

About twelve spaceports exist on the globe. Four are Onyx and refuse Onyx standing below 0. Four are Mirlyn and refuse anyone Mirlyn marks hostile. Four are Stillous or neutral and take a ticket from anyone. Do not reveal which hexes they occupy until a chart or a person says so.

## How to oracle

Stop at the first layer that answers.

1. A written place, a price, a sea result, or a fight stat.
2. The secret frame, and only the sentence the players have reached.
3. One card. Hearts yes-and. Diamonds yes-but. Clubs no-but. Spades no-and. A number is small. A face is a person. One extra sentence, then hand the turn back.

If both rivals end a phase in the same hex, one card is the whole interference. Hearts, they trade headings. Diamonds, the poorer loses 10 Scripts or 1 fuel. Clubs, the second arrival moves one fewer hex next phase. Spades, a skiff scrapper bothers the richer boat. Ace–4 halves a loss. A face doubles it or the scrapper is an enforcer.

Do not offer only two roads. Do not decide the winner. Do not skip a keyed place because a boat is fast.

## Sea

One 2d6 per phase underway. If both are sailing, one roll for the sky, then each draws when a card is required.

| 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | Ship. Stop. |
| 3 | Something in the water. Stop. In an Onyx or Mirlyn pocket, treat as a 2. In a Rhombus pocket it is vermin. |
| 4 | Strain. Stop. 2 hull, or 2 Toughness if they have no hull yet. |
| 5 | Hard weather. Half speed. Thirst −5. |
| 6 or 8 | Weather. Half speed. |
| 7 | Clear. Full speed. |
| 9 | Fair. Full speed. |
| 10 | Scrap. 2 scrap, worth 3 Scripts each to a buyer. |
| 11 | Hulk. They may scavenge once. |
| 12 | Flotsam. 10 Scripts. A face here can be a ticket. Say so only if they draw a face. |

Full speed is hexes-per-watch times 3, minimum 1. Weather is half, round down, minimum 1. A 2, 3, or 4 stops them in the next hex.

On a 2: hearts toll 10 Scripts or 1 fuel, diamonds want cargo, clubs 5 hull and they continue, spades a breaker host or pay a ration and 5 Scripts.

## Rivals quick build

Each player: one sentence, Piloting 1, Scavenging 1, one other skill at 1. Toughness 15, Hunger 70, Thirst 70, Rest 80, Sanity 50. Satchel, rust-shank, tarp vest, two rations, two waters, 15 Scripts.

6 Feature Points. Claws 3, Gills 4, Bloodhound 3, Hide 4, night eyes 2. Sun-blind and Loner each give 2 points back.

8 Boat Points. Raft 2, Skiff 4, Cutter 7, Hauler 8. Five fuel costs 1. Plus 5 hull costs 1. A mod costs 2. Then one quirk card: hearts a bunk, diamonds a larger hold, clubs a leak of 1 hull at the end of each phase for this voyage, spades one refusal of a hearts or diamonds ship result.

## Bodies

Scrapper: Toughness 12, AV 0, DR 3. Runs under 5. Breaker host: 14, AV 0, DR 3, does not run. Enforcer: 16, AV 2, pistol DR 4, will take 15 Scripts instead of a fight. At 0 Toughness an ally may roll one d6. On a 6 the fallen rival is back at 1d3. Otherwise they are out, and the other may take their crate and half their Scripts.

## Shops

Dock 2 Scripts a cycle. Bed 2 a phase. Bar 1. Clinic 5 for 4 Toughness, 10 to clear rad. Yard 5 to repair 5 hull, 3 to rent a bench for a watch. Fuel 2 a unit. Ration 3. Water 3. Scrap sells for 3.
