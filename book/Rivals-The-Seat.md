# The Seat

Not the rivals session. This was a closed two-road drill. The open race, the chip, and the satellite are in `book/SECRET-Rivals-Frame.md`. Do not open that file until you are both at the table and Muse is the one reading it.

A rivals race for two. About twenty minutes to make the people and the boats, then one day on the water. Four phases. Stop when the fourth phase ends even if nobody has paid.

You do not need the rest of the book open. You need Draft 3 only if a fight starts or a sea roll needs the long table. Those two reminders are at the bottom of this sheet.

Neither of you is the game master. This page is.

## The race

One Stillous seat leaves Port Gate at the end of Deep Night. It costs 40 Scripts. There is one seat. Veth does not bargain.

You both start at Port Dundy, globe hex 14, with 15 Scripts and a boat you just built. First to Veth's desk with 40 Scripts takes the seat. If you arrive on the same phase, draw one interference card. That card decides who reaches the desk first.

If the day ends and neither of you has paid, nobody flies. That is a finished session.

## Make a person

Skip the full closet and the five reputation draws.

- One sentence: what you are, and why you want off the water.
- Skills: Piloting 1, Scavenging 1, and one other skill at 1.
- Meters: Toughness 15, Hunger 70, Thirst 70, Rest 80, Sanity 50, Bio 10, Battery 0.
- Gear, already packed: satchel, rust-shank, tarp vest, two rations, two waters, 15 Scripts.
- 6 Feature Points from the short list.

| Feature | Cost |
|---|---|
| Claws, DR 2 | 3 |
| Gills | 4 |
| Bloodhound, and you know Heightened Alertness | 3 |
| Toughened Hide, +1 AV, +2 Toughness | 4 |
| Night eyes | 2 |
| Sun-blind, gives you 2 points back | +2 |
| Loner, gives you 2 points back, Barter −1 | +2 |

Maneuvers: if you took Bloodhound, one is Heightened Alertness. Pick the other, or pick both, from Quick Step and Power Strike.

## Make a boat

8 Boat Points. Then draw one quirk card.

| Buy | Points | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Raft | 2 | 10 hull, AV 0, 1 hex per 2 watches, no fuel |
| Skiff | 4 | 20 hull, AV 1, 1 hex a watch, tank 10 |
| Cutter | 7 | 35 hull, AV 2, 2 hexes a watch, tank 10 |
| Hauler | 8 | 60 hull, AV 3, 1 hex a watch, tank 10 |
| 5 fuel | 1 | Powered hulls only. A full tank is 10, so two buys fill it. |
| +5 hull | 1 | |
| A mod | 2 | Draw a second card. Hearts: ignore the first toll. Diamonds: +20 Scripts of space, no extra coin. Clubs: you may reroll one sea die today. Spades: the gun. Once, you may refuse a hearts or diamonds ship result. |

Quirk card, drawn after you spend:

| Suit | Quirk |
|---|---|
| Hearts | An extra bunk. No number. Someone will ask for it. |
| Diamonds | Hold is half again as large. |
| Clubs | Leak. Lose 1 hull at the end of every phase. |
| Spades | The gun, if you did not already buy it. Same one-time refusal. |

A powered boat with no fuel moves like a raft. Fuel is 2 Scripts a unit at any D.

Speed for this race: a clear phase on open water moves you hexes-per-watch times 3, and at least 1. Weather moves half of that, round down, at least 1. On a 2, 3, or 4 you only make the next hex and then deal with it. You stop when you enter a named place. A fast boat can cross empty water. It cannot skip Black Ribs, Canary, or Gate.

## The course

Draw nine hexes. Dundy on the left. Gate on the right. Two roads between them.

```
                 Canary —— N2 —— N3
                /                   \
Dundy —— E1 —— Black Ribs —— E2 —— Gate
```

East is three sails: E1, Black Ribs, E2. North is four: the water under Canary, Canary, N2, N3. A cutter on a clear phase can swallow more than one open hex. A raft will not see Gate today unless the weather is kind and you never stop. That is legal.

Keyed hexes are the places below. Open hexes are only the sea roll.

## How a phase goes

1. Both say the next hex out loud.
2. If either of you is on an open hex, roll one 2d6 for the sky. Each of you who is sailing draws your own card when the table wants one.
3. Move. Then play the place, if it has one.
4. Same hex, or you spent the phase on their wake: one interference card.

Do the shops and the wreck with the small rules under each map. Hunger, thirst, and rest still fall at the end of Deep Night: −20, −20, −50. Eat and drink when you are standing still. A ration is +25 Hunger. A water is +25 Thirst. A nap is one Action Turn, +5 Rest, and you can be found.

## Sea roll

| 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | A ship. Stop. Draw. |
| 3 | Something in the water. Stop. Draw. |
| 4 | Strain. Stop. Lose 2 hull, or 2 Toughness if you want the hull spared. |
| 5 | Hard weather. Half speed. Thirst −5. |
| 6 or 8 | Weather. Half speed. |
| 7 | Clear. Full speed. |
| 9 | Fair. Full speed. |
| 10 | Scrap. Full speed. Gain 2 scrap. Hest or Moss pays 3 Scripts each. |
| 11 | A hulk. Stop. You may run AC-01 once, then it is picked clean. |
| 12 | Flotsam. Full speed. Gain 10 Scripts. |

On a 2, draw. Hearts: a toll of 10 Scripts or 1 fuel. Diamonds: they want your scrap or your crate. Clubs: lose 5 hull and keep going. Spades: a breaker host. Fight, or give them one ration and 5 Scripts and they let the hex go.

On a 3, draw. Hearts: lose a watch going around, no fight. Anything else: a skiff scrapper. Fight, or give them 10 Scripts.

## Interference

| Suit | Sharing the hex |
|---|---|
| Hearts | Trade headings. No harm. |
| Diamonds | The poorer boat loses 10 Scripts or 1 fuel. |
| Clubs | The boat that arrived second moves one fewer hex next phase. |
| Spades | A skiff scrapper bothers the richer boat. |

Ace–4: halve a loss. Face: double it, or the scrapper is an enforcer.

## Dundy

You start on the D. Hest is in the B. The yard is the K.

```
D  .  B  .  D
.  T  H  .  .
+  .  K  .  W
.  .  .  .  .
D  .  X  .  D
```

Hest, Timberite, counts a coin twice. Buys scrap at 3 Scripts. Sells fuel at 2 a unit, rations at 3, water at 3. If you tell Hest which road the other boat took, Hest pays 5 Scripts for the sentence. That is the lever. It does not move their boat. It only pays you.

The X is a shut warehouse. Ignore it today.

Leaving the D onto E1 or toward Canary is the first sail.

## Black Ribs

A derelict on the east road. One visit. Then it is empty.

```
+  .  +  .  .
.  +  D  +  .
+  .  X  .  +
.  .  .  .  .
.  .  D  .  .
```

The sealed crate is in the X. Getting it is AC-02: one Action Turn, roll a d6.

| d6 | The hatch |
|---|---|
| 1 | Jammed. An enforcer's launch is one phase behind you. They are not here yet. |
| 2–3 | It costs a second Action Turn, then it opens. |
| 4–5 | Open. You may slot a maneuver if you have one that fits a door. Power Strike does not. Quick Step means you are out before anyone counts you. |
| 6 | Open and quiet. |

The crate is worth 25 Scripts at Gate, or 15 if you sell it to Moss at Canary. It is 2 kg. It rides in the hold.

You may also scavenge once. One Action Turn. Roll a d6. 1–2: lose 1 Toughness, gain 1 scrap. 3–4: 1 scrap. 5: 2 scrap and a water. 6: 8 Scripts and a charge cell.

```
.  .  .  .  .
```

Moss buys the Black Ribs crate for 15. Buys scrap at 3. Sells fuel, rations, and water at the same prices as Hest.

One passenger is waiting on the D. Carry them to Gate and Moss has already paid you 20 Scripts here, in advance. If you are not at Gate by the end of the day, you owe the 20 back and the passenger is your problem. They take a bunk. A hearts quirk already has the bunk. Otherwise they ride and you do not get a nap.


Veth sits in the X. Stillous. The seat is 40 Scripts. One seat. No discount, no partial payment, no Glove required. If you are short, Veth says the number you still need and looks past you.

The B will sell a drink for 1 Script and tell you nothing you do not already know.

## Bodies, if a fight happens

One Action Phase is 12 ticks. A shank acts on ticks 3, 6, 9, and 12. Moving one square is 2 ticks.

A hit: DR minus AV. Vest is AV 1. If the remainder is at least your current Toughness, you drop to 0. If it is less, roll a d6 if the remainder is 5 or 6, or just lose the remainder if it is 1 to 4. You cannot lose more than the remainder.

At 0, the other rival may roll a d6 once. On a 6 you are back at 1d3 Toughness. Otherwise you are out of the race. Your boat stays in the hex. They may take your crate and half your Scripts, round down.

**Scrapper.** Toughness 12, AV 0, pipe DR 3. They run if they end a phase under 5.

**Breaker host.** Toughness 14, AV 0, DR 3. They do not run. On a 5 or 6 on their attack die, you also lose 2 Sanity.

**Enforcer.** Toughness 16, AV 2, pistol DR 4. They would rather have 15 Scripts than your life. Offer it before the first tick if you want.

## What to write down afterward

Which road each boat took. Whether the crate moved. Whether Hest bought a heading. Which sea results actually showed up. Whether 40 Scripts was a race or a grind. Whether four phases were enough water.
