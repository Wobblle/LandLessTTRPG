# LandLess

Solitaire Survival RPG

Draft 3

This is the play copy. The 2nd-draft PDF stays the archive. Where that PDF, the sea notes, and the updates file disagreed, this book is the one that wins. Numbers are here so a session can run. Balance comes after you have played.

Two people can each play a Howling with these same rules. The second person is the ally in the downed rule.

The in-book oracle is the next pass. Until then, questions that have no card use the one-card stand-in in Chapter 12.

## What was made to agree

- Hulls use one line: Raft 10 HP / AV 0, Skiff 20 / 1, Cutter 35 / 2, Hauler 60 / 3, with the speeds and holds in Chapter 10. The sheet that said cutter 30 and hauler 45 is retired.
- A hit uses the shock roll in Chapter 4. Flat "subtract the whole Damage Rating" is retired.
- Radiation uses the burn track in Chapter 5. Losing Toughness equal to the rad level every phase is retired.
- Travel weather is the sea roll in Chapter 9. The old weather list is the effect of those results, not a second roll.
- Factions you can stand with are Onyx, Timberites, Howling Society, Rhombus, and Stillous. Mirlyn and Breakers start hostile. Rhombus is the old draft's Rambas. Mirlyn is the old draft's Murlyn.
- The skill list is the twelve names in Chapter 2. An origin that said "Survival" gives Scavenging. One that said "Perception" gives Stealth.
- A Glove is 100 Scripts. A ration restores 25 Hunger. A water pouch restores 25 Thirst.

## What you need

A pencil, the sheets in the back, one six-sided die, and a deck with the jokers removed. Damage of 7 or more wants a d8, a d10, or a d12. A d2 is a coin. A d3 is a d6 divided by two, round up.

You write the character, the pack, and the map. Nothing in this book remembers for you.

# 1. The drowned world

The year sits in the early 2600s. Earth is water, raft cities, and a little ground that is either still hot or owned by someone who shoots. Off-world is richer and colder. Dropping down is a sentence people say when they mean you will not be back.

In the 2060s the Nuclear Campfire started in Gabon. Compact fission let a town keep its own fire. The plants still needed hands. At Buni Yadi, Brailyn Spark of Spark Biogenetics built a dog that could take the radiation and run a shutdown. Those dogs are the Howlers. They were hired, paid, and identified. They were not a secret replacement for every human crew.

The seas rose later. The book does not say why. People have theories. When human states could no longer protect the plants, the Howlers opened the bio-bays, folded other animal lines into themselves, and came out the other side as the Howlings. A Howling might have claws, a long neck, hide that hates noon, or gills. The player character is one of them.

In 2130 a generation ship signaled Earth. The senders are the Stillous. About eighty thousand of them were aboard, most born on the way, already twelve hundred years out. They had been intelligent for an age before anyone helped them. The help was the Wostouf: capsules, a technology, and a contract. Using the gift means a tribute. Whether Sol still pays it is not on this page.

They also gave Earth the Glove. A Glove is a compact power source and, in this era, the premium coin. One intact Glove is worth 100 Onyx Scripts. Scripts are the daily money. Onyx mints them and taxes in them.

The globe is mostly free water. Pockets of protection sit in it. Pirates of every people work the gaps. Breakers work there too: a person locked into their own body, the motor run by a hive that does not hate and does not negotiate.

South is Onyx, and a shut land that does not take visitors. North is Mirlyn, and another shut land. Howling pockets cluster to the north and east. Rhombus pockets cluster to the west, with mechanical vermin in the water around them. Timber pockets sit in the gaps between Howling and Rhombus. Onyx will police or shake you down. Howlings rarely start a fight and will trade, usually in poor goods. Rhombus would rather not see you; their goods are scrap or high tech. Mirlyn threatens or attacks; a real trader there is rare and the goods are good. Timber is neutral, and the goods are medium.

Port Dundy is a free-water port on the southern edge of the Howling pockets, close enough to Onyx that collectors still walk the boards. Its region address is written on the chart you bought. Charts can be wrong.

# 2. Making a Howling

You need the deck and a d6. Work in order.

## Step 1. Concept

You are a Howling. Note an animal lean in a few words. It is color until a feature prices it.

## Step 2. Origin

|---|---|

| Diamonds | Resources and technology |
| Clubs | Conflict and labor |
| Spades | Loss and secrets |

**Hearts.** Ace–4: cast out of a skiff-pack. Howling Society −10. Scavenging +1. Five–10: sole survivor of a raid. A worthless memento. Sanity max +1. Face: second to a dead Howling leader. You also start with a battered boarding knife on top of the closet: sharp, DR 3, 1 kg, 1 vol, acts on Ticks 4, 8, and 12. `D6 [1–2 miss] [3–4 hit] [5 open] [6 bleed]`.

**Diamonds.** Ace–4: ruined by a bad trade. One free rumor. Five–10: pulled from a pre-calamity wreck. One scrap-electronics piece. **Clubs.** Ace–4: escaped a Mirlyn pen. Stealth +1. Five–10: fled an Onyx labor crew. You know one patrol route. Onyx −15. Face: veteran of a floater skirmish. Toughness max +2, and your current Toughness rises with it.

# 4. Doing things

## Action cards

An action card is a d6 broken into blocks. A block names the faces that trigger it. An open block can hold one talent or maneuver you declare before the roll. You may fill only one open block per card.

If the die lands on an open block you filled, you get that talent.

If it lands on an open block you left empty, and you have an unprestiged negative trait, the trait's trigger fires instead of a failure.

If it lands on an empty open block and you have no such trait, use the card's lowest bad block.

A 6 on an action-card die marks one tally on the skill that card names.

## Cards in this book

**AC-01 Scavenge.** 1 Watch. Skill: Scavenging.

`D6 [1–2: rust cut, lose 1 Toughness, gain 1 scrap metal] [3 open] [4 open] [5: 2 scrap and either 1 water or 1 ration] [6: 1 advanced electronics, 1 charge cell, and 1d10 Scripts]`

**AC-02 Force a hatch.** 1 Action Turn. Skill: Engineering, or Athletics if you are only prying.

`D6 [1: jammed. In Dundy or an Onyx pocket, the next fight in this place is an enforcer] [2–3: stalled, costs another Action Turn] [4 open] [5 open] [6: open, quiet]`

A prybar is +1 to the die on this card only, to a maximum of 6.

**AC-03 Barter.** 1 Segment. Skill: Barter.

`D6 [1: offended, prices +25% for a cycle] [2–3: list price] [4 open] [5 open] [6: 15% off, or one rumor]`

**AC-04 Day labor.** 1 Watch. Skill: Athletics.

`D6 [1: −15 Rest, earn 2 Scripts] [2–3: earn 4 Scripts] [4 open] [5 open] [6: earn 8 Scripts and 1 ration]`

## Checks without a card

Roll 2d6 and add the skill.

| Target | When |
|---|---|
| 6 | Routine |
| 8 | Risky. Failure costs a Segment or starts a hazard. |
| 10 | Extreme. Failure deals harm or raises an alarm. |

Boxcars on this roll is a tally, the same as a 6 on an action card.

## Learning

Tally marks sit beside the skill. When the marks equal your current rating plus one, erase them. At the next Full Rest the skill rises by 1.

The first time a skill reaches 3, you may prestige one negative trait. A prestiged trait does not fire on empty open slots. A later skill reaching 3 can prestige a different trait.

## Harm

Armor is the AV of the location hit. If you do not care which location, use torso AV, including Toughened Hide and sub-dermal plating.

Net damage X is weapon DR minus that AV. If X is 0 or less, the hit glances.

If X is greater than or equal to current Toughness, Toughness becomes 0.

If X is less than current Toughness, roll the die below and lose that many Toughness. The loss cannot be higher than X.

| X | Die |
|---|---|
| 1 | lose 1 |
| 2 | d2 |
| 3 | d3 |
| 4 | d4 |
| 5–6 | d6 |
| 7–8 | d8 |
| 9–10 | d10 |
| 11 or more | d12, and the loss is capped at X |

At 0 Toughness, if an ally is on the same local board or the same deck, roll a d6 once per fight. On a 6 you are back up with 1d3 Toughness. Any other result, or if you are alone, use the death table in Chapter 5.

## A fight

An Action Phase is 12 Ticks. A weapon acts on the ticks in its closet line if you are in range and you did not spend that tick moving.

Moving 1 square costs 2 Ticks. Four squares in a straight rush cost 6 Ticks. Quick Step is 1 square for 0 Ticks.

When you attack, roll that weapon's d6 code and apply harm.

After the fight, 1 Action Turn lets you roll a d6. On 3 or higher you recover 1d6 slugs, or half the harpoons you fired, plus 1 scrap metal and whatever Scripts were in a pocket.

# 5. Staying alive

At the end of each Cycle, if nothing has changed the rate:

- Hunger −20
- Thirst −20
- Rest −50

Metabolic Dormancy uses 15 for hunger and thirst. Hyper-Metabolism uses 25.

The first time in a cycle a meter crosses a line, apply it once.

| Meter | Line | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger or Thirst | 25 or under | Sanity −2. You are low. |
| Hunger or Thirst | 5 or under | Lose 1 Toughness. This particular loss will not take you below 3. |
| Hunger or Thirst | 5 or under, and you keep working | At the start of each Action Turn that is not eating or drinking, roll a d8. You pass out on a 1. Each success raises the fail range by 1, to a maximum of 1–7. Reset when that meter is above 5. |
| Hunger or Thirst | 0 | Collapse. Lose 2 Toughness. If you are above 0, use the unconscious table. |
| Rest | 35 or under | Sanity −3. You are tired. |
| Rest | 10 or under | Sanity −6 more. You are exhausted. |
| Rest | 0 | Collapse. Unconscious table. |

## Death

If you are at 0 and nobody brings you back, you die. The hex keeps a wreck marker. A later Howling who draws a face card in that same region may take the dead Howling's main weapon and a journal.

A face card on the death draw, if you want a sentence for the demise, is enough. Do not build a second table.

## Radiation

Each phase you spend in a hot place, add that place's Rad Level to your Rad-Burn. A scrubber can reduce the amount added.

| Rad-Burn | Effect |
|---|---|
| 5 to 9 | Nausea. Physical checks −1. Bio max −3. |
| 10 or more | Lose 1 Toughness per Watch until a Rad-Purge or a clinic. |

A Rad-Purge clears nausea, or it restores 2 Toughness that radiation took. It does not empty the track. A clinic visit for 10 Scripts clears the track.

A global hex is a whole region. You sail the region map. You walk a local cluster. The ship does not enter the streets. It stays on a D at the edge of the cluster.

## Globe

One flower. Put a hex in the center. Count eight hexes out from each side of that center. Each of the six sides of the big hex is nine hexes long. That sheet holds 217 hexes. Each of those 217 is a region, or a shut land that you do not sail.

Number a hex by ring and side when you need an address. Ring 0 is the center. Rings 1 through 8 go outward. A ring of radius R holds 6 times R hexes. Sides, clockwise from north: N, NE, SE, S, SW, NW. Count along a side starting at 1. The center is `0`. Any other hex is `R-SIDE-N`, such as `3-S-2`.

Most hexes are free water. Shut land is permanent and is not a normal region: Mirlyn holds a block of the far north, Onyx a block of the far south. Howling pockets cluster north and east. Rhombus pockets cluster west. Timber pockets sit in the gaps between those two. About a dozen region hexes on the whole globe are spaceports. Four answer to Onyx and refuse a boat with Onyx standing below 0. Four answer to Mirlyn and refuse anyone Mirlyn has marked hostile. Four are Stillous or neutral and take a ticket from anyone. You do not know which hexes those are until a chart or a person tells you, and either may be lying.

You do not ink a global hex until you enter it, buy a chart of it, or someone claims they have been there. Write the cycle next to anything you ink.

## Region

A region is the flower inside one global hex. Each face of that flower is four hexes long. The sheet holds 37 hexes. Columns 1 to 7, rows A to G. D4 is the center.

Columns, west to east:

- 1 holds C1, D1, E1
- 2 holds B2 through F2
- 3 holds A3 through G3
- 4 holds A4 through G4, and D4 is the center
- 5 mirrors 3
- 6 mirrors 2
- 7 holds C7, D7, E7

The first time you enter a region, date the sheet with the cycle. Place any secret that named this region, in the sector it named: center, N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, or NW. Then draw four cards for other points of interest, minus one card per secret you placed, and never below zero. A rumor from the closet draws one extra. Every remaining hex is open water until something happens there.

Suit is the kind. Hearts, a berth. Diamonds, a market. Clubs, trouble. Spades, closed.

Rank is how the place sits on this region. Ace through 4 occupies one region hex and opens a local cluster of 3 hexes. Five through 10 occupies two region hexes and opens a cluster of 6. A face occupies three region hexes and opens a cluster of 9. Port Dundy is a face: nine local hexes, three region hexes. Take the extra region hexes from empty neighbors. If you cannot, the place stays smaller.

## Local

A local is not a flower. It is a cluster. Start with one hex. Each new hex shares a side with one you already drew. Stop at 3, 6, or 9. One hex on the outer edge is the D where the ship waits. The other hexes are the streets, the yards, and the wreck.

Deal one card per local hex to fill it.

| Suit | Ace–4 | 5–10 | Face |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hearts | D | B | H |
| Diamonds | T | W | K |
| Clubs | + | K | X |
| Spades | + | X | X |

Walking into a neighboring local hex costs 1 Action Turn. That is not a sea roll. Casting off from the D puts you back on the region map, on one of the region hexes that place occupies.

## Old sheets

Land marked L stays where you put it. A city, a compound, and a shut coast are land. Their buildings can change. Wrecks, raft camps, patrols, and unanchored berths are not land.

If you return to a sheet and 7 or more cycles have passed since its date, keep the land and deal new cards for everything that is not land. Date the sheet again. A chart with no date is a rumor. Copying it does not make it true.

# 8. Who is in the water

Use these when a local cell, a ship result, or a fight needs a body. Ranges are Touch, Near (a few squares), and Far.

**Barnacle rats.** Toughness 8, AV 1. They act on Ticks 3, 6, 9, and 12. Jaws DR 1. `D6 [1–2 miss] [3–4 hit] [5–6 frenzy]`. Frenzy spoils one ration or one water. They scatter if a single hit's DR is 4 or more.

**Skiff scrapper.** Toughness 12, AV 0. Acts on Ticks 5 and 10. Pipe DR 3. `D6 [1–2 miss] [3–5 hit] [6 grapple]`. Grapple adds 2 Ticks to your next action. If they are under 5 Toughness at the end of a phase, they run on a d6 of 4 or higher.

**Breaker host.** Toughness 14, AV 0. Acts on Ticks 4, 8, and 12. DR 3. `D6 [1 miss] [2–4 hit] [5 screech] [6 grip]`. Screech is Sanity −2. Grip deals 2 damage that ignores armor and the target does not move next phase. They do not flee. They stop at 0.

**Port enforcer.** Toughness 16, AV 2. Acts on Ticks 4, 8, and 12. Pistol DR 4, capacity 6. `D6 [1–2 miss] [3–4 hit] [5 suppress] [6 crit]`. Suppress means you do not move next phase. Near crates they have Cover.

**Mirlyn razor.** Toughness 18, AV 1. Acts on Ticks 3, 6, 9, and 12. Talons DR 4, sharp. `D6 [1 miss] [2–4 hit] [5 bleed] [6 rake]`. Bleed is 1 damage at the start of each of the next 2 phases. Rake permanently drops 1 AV from one worn piece. Once a phase they may move 3 squares for 0 Ticks.

**Incinerator swarm.** Toughness 10, AV 3. Acts on Ticks 6 and 12, and moves 2 squares toward metal. On contact it clamps. The next Tick it deals DR 10 to whoever it is on, or 2 hull strain if it is on a boat. Defuse is Engineering or Demolitions, TN 8, 1 Action Turn, and you must be in the hex with it.

**Breaker stalker.** Toughness 28, AV 2. Acts on Ticks 4, 8, and 12. Scythe DR 6. `D6 [1 miss] [2–3 hit] [4 shock] [5 drain] [6 bite]`. Shock is Sanity −4 and you lose 3 Ticks. Drain removes 5 Battery. Bite gives you Breaker Trauma if you do not already have it. Optics are unreliable in its hex: ranged checks are −1.

**Onyx wraithlion.** Toughness 35, AV 3. Acts on Ticks 3, 6, 9, and 12. Once a phase it may move 4 squares and knock you prone. Jaws DR 7, and the first 2 points of AV do not count. `D6 [1 glance] [2–3 hit] [4–5 armor bite] [6 decapitate]`. Armor bite destroys 1 worn AV. Decapitate is net damage 10, then the shock roll on top of that only if you are somehow still up. A called shot on the vents is Ranged, TN 10, and that hit ignores AV.

Do not open with a stalker or a wraithlion. They are what a spade face becomes after the table has a boat and a reason.

# 9. Sailing

You do not start with a boat. A berth is a hearts cell, a barter, and a course someone else already chose. You roll the sea for each phase you stay aboard. Getting off early is a check or a jump.

Pace is measured in watches. The event is not.

Once per phase you spend underway, roll 2d6. Then move. Your hull's speed is hexes per watch. A phase has 3 watches, so a clear phase moves speed times 3 hexes, and at least 1 hex. A raft's speed is half a hex a watch, so a clear phase moves 1 hex.

| 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | A ship. Stop after 1 hex. Draw. |
| 3 | Something in the water. Stop after 1 hex. Draw. In an Onyx or Mirlyn pocket, treat this as a 2 instead. In a Rhombus pocket it is vermin, still a 3. |
| 4 | Strain. Stop after 1 hex. Draw. |
| 5 | Hard weather. Move half the phase, round down, at least 1. Draw. |
| 6 | Weather. Move half. Draw. |
| 7 | Clear. Move full. No card. |
| 8 | Weather. Move half. Draw. |
| 9 | Fair. Move full. Draw only if the card would have been a face; if you want the omen, draw, read one sentence, and sail on. |
| 10 | Scrap. Move full. Draw. Taking it costs the next watch. |
| 11 | A hulk. Stop in that hex. Draw. |
| 12 | Flotsam. Move full. Draw. |

A face on hard weather also stains the next phase: roll that 2d6 at −1, and do not go below 2.

## The card

Suit is who or what. Rank is how much. Ace–4 is small. Five through 10 is a working size. A face is large.

**On a 2.** Hearts hail for a toll or a trade. Diamonds want cargo. Clubs want the hull. Spades want you. In free water, spades are Breakers and every other suit is pirates. Inside a pocket, the pocket's people are who hailed, and the suit is only the mood.

**On a 3.** Hearts an animal. Diamonds a wreck-machine. Clubs vermin. Spades a body that is not steering itself. Ace–4 you can go around and lose one watch. Five through 10 it is in this hex. A face hits: use the closest body in Chapter 8, and an incinerator swarm if the suit was diamonds or clubs.

**On a 4.** Hearts a line. Diamonds a leak. Clubs the pump. Spades a person hurt, lose 2 Toughness. Ace–4 you still move next phase. Five through 10 costs a watch of repair first. A face marks the hull for 5 damage, or costs you 2 Toughness if you do not own the hull.

**On a 5, 6, or 8.** A number card of 6 or 8 is rain and wind and nothing else. A 5, or any face, uses the suit. Hearts: toxic drizzle, thirst −5 extra this cycle, and tarp or leather AV is 0 until the next cycle. Diamonds: hail and a gale, this phase already moved half, and a d6 of 1 or 2 is 1 hull strain. Clubs: the same toxic drizzle. Spades: glare or a phosphor fog. Until the phase ends, checks to find anything are TN +2, and in a fight you only see 2 squares.

**On a 10, 11, or 12.** Hearts is food or water. Diamonds are parts. Clubs are a person. Spades are a sealed thing; do not open it on deck without AC-02. Ace–4 is a handload. Five through 10 is a crate. A face on a 12 can be a hull, generated with Chapter 10. A face on an 11 is a hulk you might keep if you can move it.

There is no boarding procedure yet. On a 2, if you have any say, choose one. Run: spend the rest of the phase and move one hex back. Pay: lose one crate, or all the rations in sight. Stay: mark 5 hull strain and leave the fight unplayed.

# 10. Hulls

| Hull | HP | AV | Speed | Hold | Mod slots | Fuel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raft | 10 | 0 | 1 hex per 2 watches | 50 kg / 20 vol | 0 | none |
| Skiff | 20 | 1 | 1 hex per watch | 150 kg / 60 vol | 1 | tank 10 |
| Cutter | 35 | 2 | 2 hexes per watch | 300 kg / 120 vol | 2 | tank 10 |
| Hauler | 60 | 3 | 1 hex per watch | 1000 kg / 500 vol | 3 | tank 10 |

Generate a found hull with two cards. The first suit is the hull: hearts raft, diamonds skiff, clubs cutter, spades hauler. The first rank is the condition: ace–4 failing, 5–10 working, face sound. The second card is a quirk: hearts extra bunks, diamonds a larger hold (add half the listed hold again), clubs a leak of 1 hull at the end of every cycle, spades a gun you cannot fire until a later draft says how.

Failing hulls have three quarters of the listed HP, round down. Sound hulls have +5 HP and one extra mod slot.

A powered hull spends 1 fuel per phase underway. At 0 fuel, a skiff, cutter, or hauler moves as a raft until it refuels. Rafts never spend fuel.

A deck gun, if you mount one later, is DR 6 against hulls and spends 1 scrap as ammunition. It does not fire in this draft. The slot can still be written down.

If a fight at sea cannot be avoided and you refuse to run or pay, use this and then stop.

- Escape: 2d6 plus Piloting. Skiff needs 7, cutter needs 6, hauler and raft need 9. Success ends it. Failure is one hit of 6 hull minus their AV, and then you are boarding whether you wanted to or not.
- Trade shots: each hull that fires deals 6 minus the target AV to hull HP. At 0 hull the boat founders in 3 watches.
- Board: the next scene is a fight on the deck, personal scale, Chapter 4.

# 13. Rivals

Co-op is later. This mode is for two Howlings who both want the same thing. Neither of you is the game master. The sheet you copied before you sat down is the game master. The card you draw when the sheet is silent is the oracle.

## Before anyone plays

The person preparing the campaign fills this frame. A ready frame is a worked example. The first one is The Seat, in `book/Rivals-The-Seat.md`.

| Line | Write |
|---|---|
| Goal | One sentence. Who wins, and why the second arrival loses. |
| Clock | What the world is doing while you sail. Do not cut the session off for time. |
| Price | What a ticket costs, and what else can stand in for one. |
| Start | The local cluster you both begin on. |
| Ports | Which kinds of spaceport will take which standing. |
| Lever | One thing a rival can do to the other that is not a fight. |
| Secret | The facts the table learns only by arriving. Do not read them ahead. |

## A phase

Both of you play every phase. Say your heading before either of you rolls.

1. If you are sailing an open hex, roll the sea. If both of you are sailing, roll one 2d6 for the weather and then each draw your own card when the table wants a card.
2. Move. Keyed hexes use the place written on the sheet. They do not get a second random point of interest.
3. If you end in the same hex, or you spent the phase following the other boat, draw one interference card.

| Suit | What sharing a hex does |
|---|---|
| Hearts | You trade headings. No one is hurt. |
| Diamonds | Whoever has fewer Scripts loses 10, or 1 fuel if they would rather. |
| Clubs | Whoever arrived second moves one fewer hex next phase. |
| Spades | A third boat is here. Skiff scrapper. They bother whoever has more Scripts. |

Ace through 4: halve a loss, round down. A face: the loss is doubled, or the third boat is an enforcer.

Then stop. Do not narrate a second consequence.
