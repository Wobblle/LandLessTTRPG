# Playtest primer

Read this. Do not open `SECRET-Rivals-Frame.md`. That file is for Muse, at the table, so both of you hear the race at the same time.

This primer is a practice loop. The places in it are not the rivals voyage. Throw the practice sheet away when you understand the rolls.

## What a session actually is

You draw a map that might be wrong. You sail it. You find out what was real. The book already rolls the sea, the prices, and the fights. A card is only for a question those three refuse.

One phase while you are underway:

1. Say the next region hex.
2. Roll 2d6.
3. Move, or stop, from the table below.
4. If you entered a place, use the place. If you asked a question the place does not answer, draw one card. Hearts, yes and a small extra. Diamonds, yes and it costs something you can point at. Clubs, no and a worse option remains. Spades, no and it gets sharper. Then stop talking.

| 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | A ship. Stop in the next hex. |
| 3 | Something in the water. Stop. |
| 4 | Strain. Stop. Lose 2 hull. |
| 5 | Hard weather. Half speed. Thirst −5. |
| 6 or 8 | Weather. Half speed. |
| 7 | Clear. Full speed. |
| 9 | Fair. Full speed. |
| 10 | Scrap. 2 scrap. A buyer pays 3 Scripts each. |
| 11 | A hulk. You may scavenge once. |
| 12 | Flotsam. 10 Scripts. |

Full speed for the phase is the boat's hexes-per-watch times 3, and at least 1. Weather is half of that, round down, at least 1. You stop when you hit a place. You do not sail through a city.

On a 2, draw. Hearts, pay 10 Scripts or 1 fuel. Diamonds, they want a crate or your scrap. Clubs, lose 5 hull. Spades, a breaker. Fight, or pay a ration and 5 Scripts.

Walking inside a cluster costs 1 Action Turn a hex. The ship stays on the dock hex. Hunger −20, thirst −20, and rest −50 happen at the end of the day, not in the middle of a practice phase.

## The three maps

The globe is one flower. One hex in the middle. Eight hexes out from every side. Each side of the big hex is nine hexes long. 217 hexes. Each of those hexes is a region.

A region is a smaller flower. Each side is four hexes long. 37 hexes. Columns 1–7, rows A–G, center D4. You sail this sheet.

A place inside the region is a cluster, not a flower. A small place is 3 local hexes on 1 region hex. A middle place is 6 on 2. A city is 9 on 3. Port Dundy is a city. Start the cluster with one hex and stick each new hex to a side you already drew. One outer hex is the dock. That is where the ship waits.

Draw a hex when you enter it, when you buy a chart, or when someone swears they have been. Write the cycle on the sheet. Land stays. Cities and compounds are land. Their rooms can change. Wrecks, camps, and patrols are not land. Come back 7 or more cycles later and deal those again. A chart with no date is a rumor.

A secret names a region address and a sector: center, N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, or NW. When you generate that region, place the secret first and draw one fewer random place.

## Practice, one region

Use a boat that moves 1 hex a watch. Call the global hex `1-S-1` and date it cycle 1. No secrets. Draw four cards and place four places on the 37. Leave the rest water. Pick the nearest place, sail one phase, and build its cluster only when you enter it. That is the whole lesson: generate late, sail the region, walk the cluster, cast off from the dock hex.

If the practice felt like homework, the sea table is too slow or the cluster is too big. Write that down. Do not fix it in the middle of the real race.
