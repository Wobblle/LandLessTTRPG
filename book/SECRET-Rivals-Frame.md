# Secret rivals frame

For Muse. The players should not read this before the table. Reveal a section only when its trigger happens. Do not hint that a later section exists.

## What both players are

They escaped a Rhombus experiment dock. The sheet name is Rhombus. If they say Rambas, that is the same people. They are not Onyx and they are not Mirlyn unless a later card says they stole those papers. They start hostile to Mirlyn, and Onyx standing is 0 until they earn a crime.

## Trigger: before the boats

Tell them this much and nothing past it.

You got out of a Rhombus pen. The dock outside still has hulls and parts. What you grab, and how long you stay, is the boat. Then you run for Port Dundy. You do not yet know why Dundy matters. Do not mention a chip, a note, a satellite, or a ticket.

Each player draws one escape card. The suit is the only thing they take from it.

| Suit | What the escape left them |
|---|---|
| Hearts | Someone helped. Howling Society +5. They start with one extra ration. |
| Diamonds | They stole a purse. +10 Scripts. |
| Clubs | They were hunted. Onyx −10. Stealth is the skill they set to 1 if they had not chosen it. |
| Spades | They left someone behind. Sanity max 45. Breaker Trauma is not on the sheet unless they buy it. |

Build the boats with the quick rules in `Muse-Context.md`. The points are the time spent on that dock. Say that sentence. Do not add a clock.

## Trigger: they cast off

Generate the region that holds the experiment dock if it is not drawn. It is a Rhombus pocket. One secret is already placed: the dock itself, sector center, a 6-hex cluster, spades, closed except for the slip they stole the boat from. Draw three more places, not four. The sail to Dundy crosses free water. Do not name how many regions. Let their speed and the sea rolls decide. Dundy is a face-sized city, 9 local hexes on 3 region hexes, on the southern edge of the Howling pockets. Generate its region when they enter that global hex. Dundy is the secret of that region, sector you choose once and then write down so you do not move it. Draw three other places after it.

## Trigger: they open the box in Dundy

The box is in their hold. They find it when they first dock at Dundy, not before. Read this, then stop.

The note is from the Rhombus who kept the pen. There is a chip in each of you. In person, he can end you. From the satellite, he can end every loose chip at once. He goes up once a week and purges whoever is still running. He is offering a trade. Reach the satellite and fire the termination yourselves. Your own chip will answer to you after that. Everyone else still carrying one will not. Do that, and you are no longer a loose end. You will have done his errand.

Do not say "you must kill the other player." If they ask what the termination hits, say: every chip that has not been switched off, including the other boat, if they are still chipped. The first boat to fire it is the one that comes home.

## Trigger: they ask how to get up

A ticket is the ordinary way. It costs 80 Scripts, or a ticket already in hand. A face on a flotsam roll of 12 can be a ticket. Say that only when they actually roll it. Plunder from a ship, a bounty, or a buyer can make the 80. Do not invent a discount.

There are about twelve spaceports on the globe. Do not show a list. Four are Onyx and will not take Onyx standing below 0. Four are Mirlyn and will not take anyone Mirlyn calls hostile. Both players start able to use a Mirlyn port only if they somehow clear that hostility. Four are Stillous or neutral. A chart or a person can name one port. The chart can be wrong. If they press Veth or any Stillous clerk with a real ticket or the 80, that clerk can name one neutral port. The satellite is reached from a port, not from the water. The last leg is a sail to whichever port will actually let their boat dock.

## Trigger: someone fires the termination

The boat that does it keeps its rider. Every other chipped rival is done. Their boat remains where it was. Do not narrate a speech. Ask the table if they want another day.
