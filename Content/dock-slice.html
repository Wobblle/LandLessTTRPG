HOUSE · SESSION 1 · DRAFT 0 UNTOUCHED
01 · PROCEDURE

Sit down

You need this sheet, one d6, a deck with the jokers out, and a pencil. Pell is already made. The morning is already gone.

  1. Start at Midday. Roll the sky.
  2. You have six Action Turns before the cutter slips its bolts at the end of Deep Night.
  3. Pick a card. Slot one talent onto block 4 or block 5. Roll the d6.
  4. The other open block stays empty. Empty means the card's NEG, unless a talent is sitting in it.
  5. A question the cards do not answer: draw one. Suit is the verdict. Value is the twist.
  6. Eat, drink, or rest when you spend the turn to do it. At the end of Deep Night, run the bill.

Draft 0 still owns the d6 block, the Cycle bill, and the deprivation bands. This sheet owns the prices, the six-turn day, the suit verdicts, and the damage sentence: a hit subtracts its DR from Toughness. Armor reduces that DR first, down to zero. There is no second damage die.

04 · PHASE SKY · ACTION TURNS

Six turns. Three skies.

Roll 1d6 at the start of Midday, Evening, and Deep Night.

1–2Clear. Heat on wet plank. Midday stays SUN. 3–4Harsh. Midday stays SUN. Later phases: black water, no new rule. 5–6Squall. Salt wind, diesel, a distant klaxon. SUN cancels. Any NEG on Climb, Pilot, or Hazard also deals −2 Toughness.

SUN is Midday on an open boardwalk, and only while the sky is not a squall. Under the slab is shade. Evening and Deep Night are shade. Spend 1 Battery to ignore Sun-blind for the card you are on.

ACTION TURNS
ONYX TALLY · A RUNNER AT 2

A meal, a bulb, or a nap is free of an Action Turn if you are already stopped. A nap is +5 Rest and you can be found. A short rest is +25 Rest and costs 4 of the 6 turns. A full rest needs a Phase and a door. Hest's cutter is not that door until the meter is in their hand.

08 · SKY

Phase sky

Weather · free · once per phase

The wing has three lights left. One of them is the sun. One of them is a sodium lamp. One of them is a klaxon you feel in the planks.

1–2Clear. Midday is SUN on the open cards: Search, Talk, Pilot. 3–4Harsh. Same SUN rule. The hook is glare off the water, or black water after dusk. No extra penalty. 5–6Squall. SUN is gone. NEG on Climb, Pilot, or Hazard also deals −2 Toughness.

This card is the trait, not a task. It does not spend a turn. Shade cards ignore SUN: Climb, Force, Hazard, and any fight under the slab.

06 · END OF DEEP NIGHT

The bill

Draft 0: Hunger −20, Thirst −20, Rest −50, once per Cycle. This button applies that bill to the numbers above, once.

Bands, also Draft 0, fixed amounts for the slice so the session is not choosing between a die and a flat. Crossing into Low Hunger or Low Thirst, 25 or under: Sanity −2 each. Crossing into Tired Rest, 35 or under: Sanity −3. Crossing into Exhausted, 10 or under: Sanity −6 more. A single drop can cross two rest bands. If that piles on, the pile is the finding.

Bill not run.
