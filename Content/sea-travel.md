# Sea, map, and the cipher

Superseded for play by `book/LandLess-RPG-Draft-3.md`. Kept as the working notes that draft was built from.

Working rules. House, 8 November 2026. Draft 0 is untouched. Names follow the lore bible: Rhombus is the draft's Rambas, Mirlyn is the draft's Murlyn.

This is the core the draft never reached. Exploring, and the price of going. The dock slice is one local board. This is how you leave it.

The player still writes the character, the inventory, and the map. The sheet rolls dice and draws cards. It does not remember the voyage.

## Three scales

**Globe.** Static. Same every game. A reference sheet you copy once or keep beside the table. It does not get generated, and it does not fill in. It only says where the pockets cluster, and where the two shut lands are.

**Region.** Generated the first time you sail a cluster. Open water, a handful of points of interest, a few hexes each. You draw it. Later games can add clusters. The first sheet is enough.

**Local.** Generated when you enter a point of interest. A city that was two or three hexes on the region becomes a board of shops, beds, docks, and work. You draw that too, with any art you like. The symbols are the contract. The picture is yours.

Time from Draft 0, used as written:

- 1 watch is about 2 hours.
- 1 phase is 3 watches, about 6 hours.
- 1 cycle is 4 phases, about a day. Hunger −20, thirst −20, rest −50 still hit at the end of the cycle.

## The globe

Most of the sheet is free water. Pockets are protected spots, not countries. Pirates of every people work the water between them. Breakers drift there too: bodies locked to a headless hive, moving like one thought.

## Region, then local

When you decide to sail a cluster, sketch a small hex field. Seven hexes across is enough. Drop four points of interest by drawing one card each. The rest of the hexes are water. Leave them blank.

The card builds the point:

- Suit is the kind. Hearts, a berth: dock, bar, beds. Diamonds, a market: trade and work. Clubs, trouble: wreck, vermin, patrol. Spades, closed: a wall, a hive-sign, a private compound.
- Rank is the size. Ace through 4, one hex, and it does not expand. Five through 10, two hexes, and the local board is 5 by 5. Face, three hexes, and the local board is 9 by 9.

A face heart is a city. Three hexes on the region. Nine by nine once you enter: docks, bars, beds, medical, mechanical, trades, work, and the gaps between them.

Local symbols, copied into a corner of the page:

| Mark | Place |
|---|---|
| D | Dock |
| B | Bar |
| H | Beds |
| M | Medical |
| K | Mechanical |
| T | Trade |
| W | Work |
| + | Hazard |
| · | Open plank or water |

Fill a local board by dealing one card per hex you care about, in the order you draw them, left to right, top to bottom. You can leave hexes blank and only draw when the character walks in. Suit picks the row above: hearts D B H, diamonds T W, clubs + K, spades + or a closed door. Rank breaks the tie. Ace through 4 takes the first mark in the list, 5 through 10 the second, face the third. Draw the art however you want. The letter is what the rules read.

Inside a local board, travel is not the sea table. A hex of docks is an action turn and a card, the way the import wing already works. The sea table starts when the region hexes are water and you are underway.

## The sea roll

One watch moves one hex. You do not roll every watch. A full day of that would be twelve events, and the survival bill would never get a fair test. The draft got stuck because ambush and reward had to share a clock with weather.

Roll **2d6 once per phase underway.** That phase covers three watches, three hexes. A hop of one or two hexes inside sight of a port uses local cards and spends the watches. It does not use this table.

Seven is the common result on 2d6, about one roll in six. It is clear water. Nothing else. The earlier sketch also wanted 7 to be windy rain. It cannot be both. Clear sits on 7 so a day of sailing is not a fight. Weather sits on the shoulders, where it still happens often. Finds and trouble sit on the tails, about one phase in six each.

| 2d6 | Result | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Ship | Draw. Someone is on you. |
| 3 | Thing in the water | Draw. Machine, animal, or cable. |
| 4 | Strain | Draw. The hull, the line, or the pump. |
| 5 | Hard weather | Draw. The sky is the event. |
| 6 | Weather | Draw. Milder than 5. |
| 7 | Clear | No card. Mark three watches. Keep the heading. |
| 8 | Weather | Draw. Same family as 6. |
| 9 | Fair | No card unless it is a face. A face is a small omen. Read it and sail on. |
| 10 | Scrap | Draw. Salvage, if you spend the next watch. |
| 11 | Hulk | Draw. A stranded hull. |
| 12 | Flotsam | Draw. A real find. |

A day of sailing is four phases if you do not stop. Expect weather more than once, a find some days, trouble some days. Snake eyes is about one ship in nine days of sailing. That is the ambush rate. Do not add a second ambush die on top.

### What the card means

One pull. Suit is who or what. Rank is how much.

**Ship (2).**

- Hearts: they hail. Toll or trade.
- Diamonds: they want cargo.
- Clubs: they want the hull.
- Spades: they want you. In free water, spades is Breakers. Any other suit in free water is pirates.
- Ace–4: a skiff. 5–10: a working boat. Face: a real ship.

In a pocket, the suit does not invent a new people. Onyx, Howling, Rhombus, Mirlyn, or Timber is who hails, and the suit is only their mood. In a Mirlyn or Onyx pocket, treat a rolled 3 as a 2. The thing in the water was a patrol. Do not also shift the die. One bump is enough. Rhombus reads a 3 as vermin, not as a ship.

**Thing in the water (3).** Hearts animal. Diamonds wreck-machine. Clubs vermin. Spades a body that is not steering itself. Ace–4 you can go around and lose one watch. 5–10 it is in the hex. Face it hits.

**Strain (4).** Hearts sail or line. Diamonds leak. Clubs the pump. Spades someone aboard is hurt. Ace–4 ugly, still moving. 5–10 a watch of repair before the next phase. Face: mark the boat, or mark yourself for −2 toughness if you do not own one.

**Weather (5, 6, 8).** The rank only scales it. Ace–4 a passing front. 5–10 it lasts the phase. Face it lasts into the next phase too, and you roll that next phase at −1 to the total, down to 2. Suit colors a 5 or a face only. Hearts rain. Diamonds hail. Clubs toxic rain. Spades a glare or a whiteout. A 6 or an 8 with a number card is just rain and wind. Write the hook, spend the watches, sail.

**Scrap, hulk, flotsam (10, 11, 12).** Suit is the cargo's family. Hearts food or water. Diamonds parts. Clubs a body or a prisoner. Spades a sealed thing you should not open on deck. Rank is bulk. Ace–4 a handload. 5–10 a crate. Face a boat's worth, or the boat itself on a 12.

You still do not have a boarding battle. If you own a hull and you roll a 2, choose one and write it: run, spend a phase and lose one hex back the way you came. Pay, lose the crate or the rations they can see. Or mark strain and leave the fight for the combat chapter. Do not invent the battle at the table.

## Drifter, then a boat

The early game is not boat ownership. You go where the flow is. A berth is a hearts local, a conversation, and a course the captain already chose. You roll the sea for the phases you stay aboard. Getting off early is a local card or a jump.

Personal carry stays the draft. Weight and volume on your body. Someone else's hold is not your inventory.

A boat is mid-game. You get one from a face on a 12, a hulk you keep, or a purchase once trade lists exist. Generate it with two cards.

- First suit is the hull. Hearts raft. Diamonds skiff. Clubs cutter. Spades hauler.
- First rank is the condition. Ace–4 failing. 5–10 working. Face sound.
- Second card is the quirk. Hearts extra bunks. Diamonds a hold. Clubs a leak that returns every cycle. Spades a gun you cannot fire until the battle rules exist. The gun is a fact, not a system.

Speed, until a better rule earns it: a raft moves one hex per two watches. A skiff moves one hex a watch. A cutter moves two. A hauler moves one, and it is the first hull with vehicle storage. Cargo on a hauler uses the draft's vehicle storage, still unnumbered. Write what it holds. When the number feels wrong, that number is the next rule.

Crew, upgrades, production, and shop prices are not in this pass. A local T or K is where they will live. Crafting stays the draft's three gates: the skill, the material, the station. No recipe list until a station has been stood in.

## The cipher

Hidden lines, not a second plot. The word bank is public and dull. A word enters your log only when two facts line up. One fact is weather. Two facts are a code.

The bank is three pages, thirty-six words each, numbered 01 to 36. A code is `page.word`, like `1.04`.

You receive a code only in a pocket, and only if both of these are true:

- The sea roll was a 2, an 11, or a 12.
- The card suit is that pocket's suit.

Then take that pocket's code. Once per pocket per campaign. Free water never pays a code. Pirates do not carry the archive.

| Pocket | Suit | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Howling | Hearts | 1.04 |
| Onyx | Diamonds | 1.18 |
| Timber | Clubs | 2.07 |
| Rhombus | Spades, and the roll was a 12 | 2.22 |
| Mirlyn | Spades, and the roll was a 2 | 3.11 |

Write the word into the slot for that code. When all five slots are full, read them in code order, not in the order you found them.

Rhombus and Mirlyn share spades on purpose. The roll is the second key. A wall and a vermin-pocket do not teach the same word.

## Author lock

The five codes spell, in order: HEST SOLD THE METER TWICE.

That is the dock's second sale, the one Alertness can already hear on the Talk card. The cipher does not add a new secret. It makes the player earn a sentence the wing already knew. The flood's cause stays out of the bank. Do not put it on a page.
