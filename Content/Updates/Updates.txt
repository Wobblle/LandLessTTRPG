# LandLess Core Play Aids, Symbols, and Sheet Templates

## Section 1: Universal Symbol Glossary &amp; Notation

### 1.1 Local Board &amp; Map Cell Symbols

* **D (Dock):** Mooring point, gangways, water access.
* **B (Bar / Saloon):** Social interactions, rumors (AC-04), purchasing food/drink/stims.
* **H (Beds / Habitation):** Berth, rental rooms; safe Full Rest (+100 Rest for 5 Scripts).
* **M (Medical Clinic):** Trauma surgery, cybernetic surgery, Rad-Purge treatments.
* **K (Mechanical Bay):** Crafting bench, drydock, repairs, upgrade installation.
* **T (Trade Stall):** General merchant, surplus buyer, commodity exchange.
* **W (Work / Labor):** Day-labor contracts (AC-03), dock shifts for Scripts.
* **\+ (Hazard / Wreck):** Environmental danger, toxic leak, scavenger salvage (AC-01).
* **· (Open Plank / Water):** Unclaimed decking, narrow catwalks, seawater transit.
* **[X] (Closed / Security):** Locked bulkhead, biometric gate (AC-02), private compound.

### 1.2 Combat &amp; Action Resolution Symbols

* **Tk (Tick):** Sub-second combat timing unit (12 Ticks = 1 Action Phase).
* **APh (Action Phase):** One combat round (12 Ticks).
* **DR (Damage Rating):** Raw damage value inflicted by a successful hit.
* **FR (Fire Rate / Speed):** Tick frequency of attack (e.g. FR 4 Tk acts at Ticks 4, 8, 12).
* **AV (Armor Value):** Subtracted directly from incoming weapon DR.
* **[Open]:** Action Card slot accepting positive Talents or triggering Negative Traits.
* **TN (Target Number):** Difficulty for ad-hoc 2d6 + Skill checks (Routine: 6, Risky: 8, Extreme: 10).

### 1.3 Playing Card Interpretations

* **Hearts:** Berth (Dock/Bar/Beds) | Toll/Trade | YES, AND... (Advantage) | Social memory legacy
* **Diamonds:** Market (Trade/Work/Tech) | Cargo demand | YES, BUT... (Cost/Delay) | Gear stash legacy
* **Clubs:** Trouble (Wreck/Patrol/Mine) | Hull demand | NO, BUT... (Alternative) | +1 Skill legacy
* **Spades:** Closed (Wall/Gate/Threat) | Hostile/Breakers | NO, AND... (Complication) | Ghost wreck legacy

## Section 2: The Three-Tier Mapping System

### 2.1 The Map Hierarchy

1. **The Globe (Macro):** Fixed 9-hex-face reference sheet showing closed empires, regional pockets, and open free water.
2. **The Region (Meso):** 37-hex flower (4 hexes per outer side, 7 hexes across the middle). Alphanumeric offset coordinates (Columns 1-7, Rows A-G, Center at D4).
3. **The Local Board (Micro):** Square grid (5x5 or 9x9) generated when docking at a POI hex.

### 2.2 Region 37-Hex Alphanumeric Coordinate Grid

* Columns 1 through 7 (Left to Right)
* Rows A through G (Top to Bottom)
* Center is D4.
* Col 1: C1, D1, E1 (and border)
* Col 2: B2, C2, D2, E2, F2
* Col 3: A3, B3, C3, D3, E3, F3, G3
* Col 4: A4, B4, C4, D4 (Center), E4, F4, G4
* Col 5: A5, B5, C5, D5, E5, F5, G5
* Col 6: B6, C6, D6, E6, F6
* Col 7: C7, D7, E7

### 2.3 Sailing &amp; Override Rules

* **Pace:** 1 Watch (2 hours) = 1 hex movement.
* **Sea Roll:** 2d6 once per Phase (every 3 Watches / 3 hexes in open water).
* **3-Hex POIs:** Face cards claim current hex as center plus 2 adjacent hexes.
* **Targeted Overrides:** Known secret coordinates (e.g., Globe 14, Region E6) override random card draws.

### 2.4 Local Board Templates

* **5x5 Small Board (Cards 5-10):** Deal cards row by row. Outer edge contains D (Docks); center contains B, T, W, H; hazard (+) placed on clubs/spades.
* **9x9 Major City Board (Face Cards):** Multiple dock slips (D), central market plazas (T, K, M), residential habitations (H), dive bars (B), and back-alley hazards (+).

# LandLess RPG Rulebook Expansion &amp; Integration Guide

## Chapter 1: Character Creation Expansions (Step 5 through Step 8)

### 1.1 Step 5: Howling Features &amp; Trait Engine

Surviving characters begin with a budget of **10 Feature Points (FP)** to spend on biological traits (Animalic) or cybernetic modifications. Taking Negative Traits grants additional FP above the 10 FP base limit. Negative traits are mechanically tethered to the **[Open Slot Trigger]** system on Action Cards.

#### Table CC3.A: Positive Animalic Features

* **Gills &amp; Webbing (4 FP):** Breathe underwater indefinitely; +1 Athletics (Swimming); ignore water movement penalties.
* **Raptor / Feline Claws (3 FP):** Natural Melee Weapon (DR 2, Sharp, 0 Wt/Vol). Grants +1 Athletics (Climbing).
* **Bloodhound Olfaction (3 FP):** +1 Scavenging / Alertness checks. Automatically grants the *Heightened Alertness* Maneuver.
* **Toughened Hide (4 FP):** Permanent +1 natural Armor Value (AV) (stacks with worn armor); +2 Max Toughness.
* **Nocturnal Tapetum (2 FP):** Ignore darkness/low-light penalties during Deep Night Phases or in unlit wrecks.
* **Scavenger's Gut (2 FP):** Max Hunger +10\. Safely consume raw or lightly irradiated food/water without HP or Sanity loss.
* **Metabolic Dormancy (3 FP):** Bio-Energy Max +5\. Hunger and Thirst deplete by 15 per Cycle instead of 20.
* **Chameleon Chromatophores (4 FP):** Spend 2 Bio-Energy to gain +2 Stealth for 1 Segment (Seg) or trigger *Cover* in combat.

#### Table CC3.B: Positive Cybernetic Features

*(Each feature increases Maximum Battery Energy, which powers special gear and actions)*.

* **Rhombus Optic Suite (3 FP, +5 Battery Max):** +1 Ranged. Spend 1 Battery to slot *Precise Aim* into an open weapon block.
* **Hydraulic Frame (4 FP, +5 Battery Max):** Carry Capacity +10 kg (to 30 kg). Spend 1 Battery for +2 DR on one melee hit.
* **Sub-Dermal Plating (4 FP, +0 Battery Max):** Passive +1 AV to Torso; +3 Max Toughness. Heavy: Athletics -1 in water.
* **Datajack &amp; Diagnostic (2 FP, +5 Battery Max):** +1 Hacking &amp; Engineering. Direct interface with pre-flood rigs and terminals.
* **Internal Rad-Scrubber (3 FP, +5 Battery Max):** Spend 1 Battery per Phase to reduce local Radiation Level damage by 1.0.
* **Micro-Welder Digit (2 FP, +5 Battery Max):** Built-in tool. Spend 1 Battery for field repairs without needing a tool kit.

#### Table CC3.C: Negative Traits (Disadvantages &amp; Trait-Slot Triggers)

* **Chronic Glitch (+3 FP):** *(Req. Cybernetics)*. **[Trigger]:** Actuator lockup; lose 2 Battery Energy or 3 Ticks.
* **Fragile Frame (+4 FP):** \-3 Max Toughness (Base 12) and -4 kg Carry. **[Trigger]:** Take 1 Bruise damage (HP).
* **Sunlight Sensitivity (+2 FP):** \-1 check penalty in clear Midday sun. **[Trigger]:** Dazzled; next action costs +1 Seg/Tick.
* **Hyper-Metabolism (+3 FP):** Hunger/Thirst drain at 25/Cycle (vs. 20). **[Trigger]:** Immediately lose 3 Hunger or Thirst.
* **Onyx Bounty / Wanted (+3 FP):** Start at -20 Rep with Onyx Empire. **[Trigger]:** Alert patrol / raise suspicion in civilized POIs.
* **Loner / Feral Wiring (+2 FP):** \-1 Persuasion and Barter. **[Trigger]:** Misread social cue; NPC disposition worsens 1 step.
* **Breaker Trauma (+3 FP):** \-10 Max Sanity (Base 40). **[Trigger]:** Phantom neural feedback; lose 2 Sanity immediately.

### 1.2 Step 6: Standardized 12-Skill Matrix

Skills are rated on a **0 to 12** scale (0 = Basic, 12 = Master):

* **Combat:** Melee, Ranged, Demolitions
* **Technical:** Engineering, Hacking, Piloting
* **Social &amp; Mind:** Barter, Persuasion, Medicine
* **Survival &amp; Covert:** Athletics, Stealth, Scavenging *(covers foraging, searching, and alertness)*

### 1.3 Step 7: Starting Tactical Maneuvers (Table CC4)

Characters start with **two** Tactical Maneuvers. Known list includes:

* **Quick Step:** Reposition 1 grid square immediately without spending Ticks, OR reduce incoming melee DR by 1 this Action Phase.
* **Bleed Attack:** *(Req. Sharp weapon or Claws)*. Successful attack inflicts 1 ignore-AV damage at start of next 2 Action Phases.
* **Focus:** Add +1 to your next attack or technical roll.
* **Cover:** Gain +1 AV while behind solid terrain.
* **Heightened Alertness:** Act 1 Tick sooner on first combat turn, or delay an enemy by 1 Tick.
* **Power Strike:** Spend 2 extra Ticks to deal +2 DR on a melee strike.
* **Distraction:** Force an opponent to retarget or lose their next 2 Ticks.
* **Precise Aim:** Eliminate hit penalties on long-range attacks.

## Chapter 2: Core Resolution, Health &amp; Progression

### 2.1 Action Card Effect Block Resolution &amp; Negative Trait Binding

* When executing an Action Card, roll 1d6 against its Effect Block Code.
* **Open Slots:** Pre-assigning a matching Talent/Maneuver activates that custom benefit on that number.
* **Negative Trait Binding:** If an `[open]` slot is left **empty/unslotted**, and the player possesses an un-upgraded Negative Trait from CC3.C, rolling that number immediately triggers the negative trait penalty instead of a standard failure.

### 2.2 Toughness, Shock Roll &amp; Lethality

1. **Net Damage (X):** `X = Weapon Damage Rating (DR) - Target Armor Value (AV)`.
2. **Lethality Check:**
  * If `X &gt;= Current Toughness`, target drops to 0 HP immediately.
  * If `X &lt; Current Toughness`, roll **1d(X)** (die size matching net damage: d2, d3, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12) and deduct the result from Toughness.
3. **Ally Pickup Rule:**
  * At 0 Toughness while solo: roll immediately on **Table SV2 (Death &amp; Demise)**.
  * At 0 Toughness with an ally/companion present: roll 1d6\. On a **6**, companion stabilizes you with +1d3 HP (usable once per encounter).

### 2.3 Skill Progression &amp; Skill Prestige

* **Skill Tally:** Rolling a natural 6 or rolling a critical effect on a skill-linked action marks 1 tally next to that skill.
* **Leveling Up:** When tallies equal `Current Skill Rating + 1`, clear tallies and increase the skill by +1 during the next Full Rest (Phase).
* **Skill Prestige:** Reaching **Rank 3** in any skill permits the player to "Prestige" one Negative Trait. It becomes mastered: empty `[open]` slots no longer trigger the disadvantage.

### 2.4 Ad-Hoc Skill Checks

When an action has no dedicated Action Card, roll **2d6 + Skill Rating** against:

* **TN 6 (Routine / Strained):** Standard task under mild pressure.
* **TN 8 (Difficult / Risky):** Complicated task; failure costs a Segment or triggers a hazard.
* **TN 10 (Extreme / Treacherous):** Near-impossible task; failure deals direct damage or triggers alerts.

### 2.5 Tactical Movement &amp; Rest Mechanics

* **Tactical Movement:** Moving 1 grid square costs **2 Ticks**. Sprinting (4 squares) costs **6 consecutive Ticks**. *Quick Step* moves 1 square for **0 Ticks**.
* **Short Rest (1 Watch / 4 ATs):** Recovers **+25 Rest** and **+2 Bio-Energy** (no HP recovery).
* **Full Rest (1 Phase / 3 Watches in Safe Berth/Bed):** Recovers **+100 Rest**, resets **Bio-Energy to Maximum**, and restores **+2 Toughness (HP)** if Hunger and Thirst are &gt; 25.
* **Post-Combat Recovery:** Spend 1 AT post-fight: roll 1d6\. On 3+, recover 1d6 Slugs or half of fired harpoons, plus 1x Scrap Metal and enemy pocket scripts.

## Chapter 5: Survival Event Tables &amp; Solo Oracle

### 5.1 Card-Deck Oracle Resolver

Ask a Yes/No question and draw 1 playing card:

* **Hearts (♥):** Yes, and... (Exceptional positive benefit)
* **Diamonds (♦):** Yes, but... (Success with minor complication or resource cost)
* **Clubs (♣):** No, but... (Failure, but a silver lining or alternative opens)
* **Spades (♠):** No, and... (Dire failure, hazard escalation, or immediate ambush)
* **Rank Modifiers:** Ace / Face Card = Extreme Twist or unexpected faction intervention.

### 5.2 Environmental &amp; Survival Tables

* **Table SV1: Unconscious Events (Draw 1 Card upon collapsing):**
  * *Hearts:* Rescued by a passing drifter or medic; wake in a berth with -10 Scripts.
  * *Diamonds:* Robbed of loose scripts and bag contents, but alive with 1 HP.
  * *Clubs:* Drifting adrift in bilge water; contract minor water-sickness (-10 Max Rest).
  * *Spades:* Captured by scavengers or Breaker thralls; must stage an immediate escape.
* **Table SV2: Death &amp; Legacy (Demise):**
  * Dying leaves a wreckage marker at current coordinates. Next character drawing a Face card in this region recovers the predecessor's primary weapon and journal.
* **Table SV4: Rad-Burn Table (When Rad Level reaches 5.0+):**
  * *5.0 - 9.0:* Nausea (-1 to all physical checks, Max Bio-Energy reduced by 3).
  * *10.0+:* Cellular collapse (Lose 1 Toughness per Watch until treated with Rad-Purge or clinic).
* **Table SV5: Weather Hazard Matrix:**
  * *Clear:* Normal travel.
  * *Toxic Drizzle:* \-1 AV to exposed leather/tarp over 1 Cycle; Thirst drain +5.
  * *Gale Squall:* Ship travel speed halved; 1d6 test to avoid taking 1 Ship Hull Strain.
  * *Bio-Phosphor Fog:* Navigation TN +2; sight range reduced to 2 squares in combat.

## Chapter 6: Vessel Operations &amp; Naval Skirmishes

### 6.1 Vessel Archetypes &amp; Base Stats

* **Raft:** Hull HP: 10 | Hull AV: 0 | Speed: 1 hex / 2 Watches | Cargo: 50 kg / 20 Vol
* **Skiff:** Hull HP: 20 | Hull AV: 1 | Speed: 1 hex / 1 Watch | Cargo: 150 kg / 60 Vol
* **Cutter:** Hull HP: 35 | Hull AV: 2 | Speed: 2 hexes / 1 Watch | Cargo: 300 kg / 120 Vol
* **Hauler:** Hull HP: 60 | Hull AV: 3 | Speed: 1 hex / 1 Watch | Cargo: 1000 kg / 500 Vol

### 6.2 The Naval Engagement Sequence (2d6 Sea Roll = 2)

When intercepted by a hostile vessel, choose one of three maneuvers:

1. **Full Burn (Escape):** Roll Piloting check (TN 7 for Skiff, TN 6 for Cutter, TN 9 for Hauler). Success breaks away. Failure takes 1 Broadside volley and forces personal combat.
2. **Broadside Exchange:** Both vessels roll deck weapons. Deal `Weapon DR - Target Hull AV` to Ship Hull HP. If Hull drops to 0, vessel begins taking water and founders in 3 Watches.
3. **Ram &amp; Board:** Close distance to 0 Ticks. Skirmish transitions immediately to personal tactical combat across deck planks.

## Chapter 7: Threat Catalog &amp; Bestiary

## Chapter 8: The Alphanumeric Hex-Mapping Overhaul

### 8.1 The Three Scales

1. **Globe (Macro):** Fixed 9-hex-face Terran globe reference.
2. **Region (Meso):** 37-Hex Flower (4 hexes per side, 7 hexes across center). Alphanumeric coordinates: Columns 1 to 7, Rows A to G. Center departure hub is **D4**.
3. **Local Board (Micro):** 5x5 (standard 2-hex POIs) or 9x9 (major 3-hex floating cities) square grid unfolded upon docking.

### 8.2 Regional Exploration &amp; Override Rules

* Draw 4 playing cards per region to populate POIs; remaining hexes are open water.
* **Suit = Type:** Hearts (Berth), Diamonds (Market), Clubs (Trouble/Wreck), Spades (Closed/Hostile).
* **Rank = Size:** Ace-4 (1 hex, no expansion), 5-10 (2 hexes, 5x5 board), Face Card (3 hexes, 9x9 city).
* **3-Hex POI Placement:** Center hex placed where drawn; claims 2 adjacent empty hexes.
* **Targeted Override Rule:** When sailing into coordinates matching a known secret or cipher location, do not draw randomly; the known secret POI automatically overrides or attaches as a hidden sub-level.
